`--cl-bone` and `--cl-dim` take their hue from `--cl-ink`, so the neutral ramp is the same material as the field. Every step of `--sl-color-white` and `--sl-color-gray-*` is derived from them.

## Focus ring

The ring is the flavor's one accent — there is no second color reserved for focus.
